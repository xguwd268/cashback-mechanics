## How Cashback Works

The shopping flow is simple, but the order of operations matters.

Start at ShopBack through the website, app, or browser extension. Search for the store you want to use and open its ShopBack page.

The merchant page should show the current cashback rate, coupon information, exclusions, and any special conditions. Rates are not universal. One retailer may offer a percentage of the purchase subtotal, while another may offer a fixed dollar amount or restrict cashback to particular categories.

Click the button that takes you to the retailer. This activates the referral session.

Do not assume that visiting ShopBack earlier in the day is enough. The click-through immediately before shopping is the part that matters for tracking.

### 3. Complete Checkout in the Same Session

Shop normally on the retailer’s website. Add products to your cart, enter shipping information, apply an eligible coupon if available, and complete payment.

### 4. Wait for the Order to Track

A qualifying order may first appear as tracked or pending. Pending cashback means ShopBack has received a tracking signal, but the merchant has not finished approving the transaction.

The amount may not be final while the order can still be returned, canceled, exchanged, or refunded.

### 5. Wait for Merchant Confirmation

The merchant reviews the order and confirms whether the referral commission is payable. Retail orders may take days or weeks, depending on the store’s return and validation process. Travel cashback can take longer because the booking may not be confirmed until after the stay, trip, or activity is completed.

### 6. Withdraw Confirmed Cashback

Once the cashback becomes confirmed and reaches the applicable withdrawal threshold, you can request payment through an available payout method.

## How Much Cashback Can You Earn?

There is no single ShopBack cashback rate. The amount depends on the merchant page that you open before shopping.

Common rate structures include:

- A percentage of the eligible purchase subtotal
- A fixed dollar amount for a qualifying action
- A new-customer promotion
- A limited-time boosted rate
- Different rates for different product categories
- A lower rate for existing customers or selected products

Cashback is often calculated on the eligible subtotal rather than the full amount paid at checkout. Shipping, taxes, gift cards, marketplace fees, subscriptions, or excluded products may not count. The exact exclusions are controlled by the retailer’s ShopBack terms.

For example, a store may advertise a percentage cashback rate but exclude shipping charges and taxes. Another store may restrict the rate to selected categories. That is why the headline rate should not be treated as a guaranteed percentage of your entire receipt.

ShopBack’s store directory also warns that rates change and that the live merchant page is the binding reference for the current offer.

## How to Avoid Missing Cashback

Most cashback problems come from tracking interruptions rather than from the basic ShopBack process. Use this checklist before placing an order:

1. Start from ShopBack immediately before shopping.
2. Check the store’s cashback terms and exclusions.
3. Enable cookies for the shopping session.
4. Avoid private or incognito browsing when possible.
5. Temporarily disable ad blockers or privacy tools that interfere with affiliate tracking.
6. Do not click another cashback site before completing checkout.
7. Avoid switching browsers, devices, or apps during the order.
8. Use coupon codes displayed by ShopBack or clearly approved by the merchant.
9. Complete payment in the same session.
10. Keep the order confirmation email and order number.

ShopBack’s support documentation specifically identifies ad blockers, disabled cookies, private browsing, device switching, competing affiliate links, and unsupported coupon codes as common reasons cashback may fail to track. Returns, cancellations, partial refunds, and excluded categories can also reduce or remove the reward later.

The coupon issue is easy to overlook. A coupon can save money at checkout but still make the order ineligible for cashback if the retailer does not approve that code for the ShopBack referral. When the two benefits are compatible, use the code shown on the ShopBack store page.

## What to Do When Cashback Does Not Appear

Do not file a missing-cashback request immediately after checkout. First, check your ShopBack activity and allow time for the transaction to appear. ShopBack recommends waiting at least 48 hours before submitting a missing cashback report, although some stores may take longer.

If the order is still missing, gather:

- Retailer name
- Order number
- Purchase date and time
- Order total
- Product details
- Confirmation email or receipt
- Screenshots of the order, if available
- The ShopBack store page or offer used

Submit the claim through the ShopBack support flow within the reporting window shown for that merchant. A missing-cashback claim does not guarantee approval, because the retailer still needs to confirm that the order was eligible and attributable to ShopBack.

If the order appears as pending, that usually means the tracking signal worked and the merchant is still reviewing the purchase. Pending is not the same as rejected, but it is also not money that can necessarily be withdrawn yet.
